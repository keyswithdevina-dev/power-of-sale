What is a power of sale?

A power of sale is when a mortgage lender sells a property after the owner has defaulted on the mortgage. In Ontario this usually happens without going through the courts.

For buyers, it can be a way into a home you might not find on the regular market. It also comes with differences you should understand before you make an offer.

The owner defaults

Mortgage payments are missed and the lender follows the required notice steps.

The lender sells

The property is listed, often on MLS, so the lender can recover what is owed.

A buyer steps in

Offers are reviewed on their terms, so it pays to know the process first.

What buyers should know before making an offer

Often sold as is

Lenders typically offer no warranties about condition, so inspections and your own checks matter.

The terms can differ

These sales often use their own conditions and paperwork. Have a lawyer review before you commit.

Price varies

Some sell below what a regular sale might bring, but there is no guarantee, so compare carefully.
